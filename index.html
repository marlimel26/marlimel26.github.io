O to be Her; A Prose Ode

For Melissa

Intro: The Heart of the Ode

When you think of her story, you think of our history. When you think of her story, you think of a journey that has given more than it has taken. A journey in which she has had to ...

This is a prose ode, the telling of love of love, love by her, love for her. A prose ode for Melissa. And this, her day, on another completion of her journey around the sun.

She is love. The love she gives. The love she exudes. The love she evokes. It is never a tiresome action to express the love that we have for her. If you know Melissa, you know that ...

And when I think of her today, I think of all that she has been to us. I think of everything that she is. She has been the center of our stories.

Melissa's story is one of struggle and of triumph. And she will tell you without shame that there have been many burdensome days.

Her Struggle and Triumph

The first thing to be her is to be one who has fought. It is easy to think, seeing her glow now, that her journey has been easy, but it has not.

Through perseverance and hard work, she was successful. She overcame. And her overcoming has been no small thing.

The One Who Has Opened

The second thing to be her is to be one who has opened. This is no small matter, when she had every reason to remain closed.

Through her continued openness, she has enabled the creation of beauty, love, and possibilities.

That she has opted to share her heart with me is a celebration warranting its own echoes; echoes of praise.

The One Who Loves Family

The third thing to be her is to be one who loves family. Her heart is large enough to carry generations, community, and chosen kin.

The love for family. The love for community. The love for her village. The love for building more, for creating new villages.

The One Who Is Committed

The fourth thing to be her is to be one who is committed. This commitment is unwavering to her people, her work, her calling, and her healing.

She is committed to her village. She is committed to us. She is committed to healing. She is committed to her calling.

Oh, to be her. What a gift that keeps on giving.

To Live Into Kindness

The fifth thing to be her is to live into kindness. Her kindness challenges, rescues, warms, and steadies.

Her kindness extends not just to those who are recipients of her ever-abiding love, but it knows no bounds.

Oh, to be her. To be kind.

To Be A Mother

Then another thing to be her is to be mother. For her, motherhood is stewardship. It is tending the future and nurturing growth.

She does many things, and she is the best at many things. But indeed, she is the best at motherhood.

Oh, to be her, to be a mother.

To Be A Wife

And then, to be her, and this is my favorite, is to be a wife. To be my wife. To be the one I have the privilege of journeying with.

She makes the giving of love look effortless. Each gesture, each smile, each moment shared, each intentional bit is love.

Oh, to be her. An ode to Melissa.

Happy birthday to her.

The Year That Has Been

It is noteworthy to reflect upon the year that has been.

Now, since this is the usual practice for most birthday events, it is not an unjust question to wonder: what has been so special about this year?

The answer is that it has been, by far, one of the most pressing and demanding seasons of this story. The year that has been has demanded a lot of us, more than we have ever had to give before.

That is noteworthy.

In the absence of her partner, who was off in pursuit of bigger dreams and a future season for the family, she bore the moment.

It was just before the departure that we learned of an impending arrival. Though she would turn out to be our greatest blessing yet, what a ball of energy and light is baby, at the time, it was frightening.

But, this little princess decided that her journey of arrival would look different and would not be dictated by any pre-baked plans. She is spontaneous, you see, and perhaps poetic even.

The point is, it was a difficult journey, one taken apart, thousands of miles apart. But she bore it. She stood through it. She watered, nourished, and poured into our arrival.

And our blessing came just in time.

Interestingly enough, right at separation's end, 16 sunrises, to be exact, she came.

And so, in the interest of abbreviating this story, the fact of the matter is that this has been a year of challenge, a year of tears, a year of loss, a year of grief. But in an oxymoronic, almost spiritually poetic way, it has also been the year of most blessings, the year in which we have gained more than we have had to grieve.

Talk about restoration. A Job restoration story.

And so it is worth praising her. It is worth reflecting, giving thanks, rendering glory, and giving praise. For though surviving the year that has been is nothing short of a miracle, God's chosen bearer of that miracle has been her.

So we celebrate her. We exist in joy for her. We delight. We give thanks.

I give thanks.

I exist in and salivate this love, this love, and how she has championed this year. Some would say, she is the real soldier.

And so, in all of it, what do we say?

Happy birthday, Mama Mel.

Happy birthday, my wife, Lizzie and Williams' mom.

Happy born day.

May our showering of love and obedient waiting upon you today begin to repay what can never be repaid: how you stood for us and showed up for us in this year that has been.

May the God of love, the Author of our story, grant many more years with you, many more years, and even countless ones in glory's hall.

We love you, Mama Mel.
